\section*{Project Team}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Project Team}

The project team consists of seven members. The work is organised into six thematic
areas reporting to the Project Manager, each led by a team lead and divided into
three sections with clearly defined responsibilities. The Project Manager is
responsible for overall coordination, planning and project control, including the
supervision of all areas and their leads.

The team composition reflects the scope of the project and covers all competence
areas required by the report: research and requirements analysis, system
development and integration, finance and project support, security and compliance,
operations and user support, and quality and continuous improvement, all
coordinated by the Project Manager. The assignments follow the actual contribution
to the report chapters so that responsibility for each area is unambiguous.

Two competence gaps are identified and consciously mitigated. First, the team has
no practising lawyer or certified ISO/IEC~27001 lead auditor; legal and
certification aspects are therefore handled in cooperation with external
specialists and certification bodies. Second, the team works part-time, so the
availability expressed in FTE is an assumption adopted for planning purposes and
must be confirmed by each member before the schedule is baselined.

\textbf{Note -- vendor team versus project team.} Table~\ref{tab:capex_zespol} in
the cost estimate (Category~A) lists the roles of the assumed implementation
contractor (project manager, architect, developers, DevOps, QA, etc.). Those roles
are pricing assumptions of the vendor used to build the CAPEX estimate; they do
not describe the composition of the student project team presented in this
section. The two layers must not be confused.
